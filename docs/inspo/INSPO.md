## Style read (first-pass, to be developed with Siddharth)

- **Medium**: screen-print / risograph / photocopy-zine — halftone dots, grain, slight misregistration; PHOTOGRAPHIC and engraved source material, not cartoons, not pencil.
- **Color**: 2–3 ink duotones/tritones per piece on one saturated field (red, chartreuse, hot pink, coral, acid palettes). Color does the emotion.
- **Composition**: album-cover confidence — one subject, dead center or hard crop; repetition as rhythm (hands, DALI type, posterized busts, eye grid).
- **Texture of feeling**: loud, ironic, a bit punk — "gentle because it's stupid": the joke/deadpan carries warmth (BLAH BLAH BLAH, flowers over eyes).
- **Obvious product hooks**: 08 (same figure in different colorways = taste DNA / people as palettes), 03 (hands pointing = the match), 01/12 (flowers over eyes = what you love becomes how you see), 09 (print culture + collage = the museum of yourself).
